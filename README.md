## 👩🏾‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Health Data Scientist** and **AI & Machine Learning enthusiast** based in London, with an MSc in Bioinformatics (Merit) and a BSc in Biomedical Science — both from Queen Mary University of London.

My work sits at the intersection of biology, data, and AI. I've applied machine learning to large-scale genomic datasets, including the **UK Biobank** (500,000+ participants), surfacing statistically significant variants linked to mood disorders. I'm passionate about using data-driven approaches to solve meaningful problems in health and medicine.

- 🔬 &nbsp;Working at the intersection of **health data, genomics, and ML**
- 🧠 &nbsp;MSc research: applying **random forests & SVMs** to predict genetic risk
- 🏥 &nbsp;Built end-to-end pipelines for **population-scale health data analysis**
- 🤖 &nbsp;AI Content Quality Reviewer — evaluating generative AI for accuracy & safety
- 🌱 &nbsp;Currently deepening expertise in **deep learning & MLOps**
- 💼 &nbsp;**Open to Data Science & Health AI opportunities in London**
